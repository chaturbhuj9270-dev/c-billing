Account deletion

Delete your account

Use this page to permanently delete your C-Billing account from the web (required by Google Play). This removes your Firebase login and the cloud profile / business data we store for your account.

You can also delete from the app: Profile → Delete account on Android, iOS, or desktop.

For accounts created with email and password. Sign in to confirm it is you, then delete.

Request a 6-digit code sent to the email on your C-Billing account, then confirm deletion. Works when you still have access to that inbox.

Phone-only accounts: open the C-Billing app → Profile → Delete account, or email support@cbilling.app from the phone number / email linked to your account.
